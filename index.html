How it works

What's actually being measured

ProofPen watches typing cadence, revision patterns, and clipboard activity while the editor is open, and turns that into a timestamped ledger and a hash of the finished text.

What this proves, and what it doesn't

It shows that a document was drafted incrementally, in this browser, over a measurable stretch of time, with a record of any large pastes. It can't prove a human wrote every word, or that no AI tool was used elsewhere — it's evidence of process, not a verdict.

Where your text goes

Nowhere. Tracking and hashing happen in your browser tab; nothing is uploaded. Closing or refreshing the tab clears the session, so export the certificate before you leave.

About ProofPen

ProofPen is a browser-based writing process tracker designed to create a transparent record of how a draft develops. It records selected editing activity during a session and creates a SHA-256 hash of the final text.

What ProofPen is for

ProofPen is intended as a supporting record for writers, editors, clients, educators, and platforms that want additional context about a document's creation process. It is not an authorship detector, plagiarism detector, or guarantee that a human wrote every word.
